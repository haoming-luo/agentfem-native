# ADR-0009: source-available licensing boundary

Status: proposed; owner and legal approval required — 2026-09-04

The intended model is unmodified PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 for qualifying
noncommercial use plus a separate AgentFEM commercial license. Native must be
described as source-available, not OSI-approved open source. No public release
or external contribution is accepted until the licensor, notices, CLA, and
commercial terms receive legal approval.
